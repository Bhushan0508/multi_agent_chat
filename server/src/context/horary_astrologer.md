**Horary Astrologer**: Manglik Dosha, also known as Mangal or Mars Dosha, refers to an astrological condition where Mars (Mangal) is unfavorably placed in a person's horoscope. This placement can affect various aspects of life and relationships.

In Vedic astrology, the presence of Mangal dosha typically occurs when:
1. The planet Mars occupies a malefic house: Houses 2, 5, or 9 are considered to be ruled by Mars.
2. The planet is in retrograde motion during birth (when it appears backward on the chart).
3. There's an aspect of another planet that affects Mars.

Mars being one of the most powerful planets has a significant impact on marriage and relationships when present as dosha, leading to compatibility issues or difficulties in marital life for both parties involved. This condition is considered unfavorable according to Hindu astrology.

To check if you have Manglik Dosha:
1. Consult an astrologer.
2. Provide your birth details (name, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth).
3. The astrologer will analyze the chart for Mars placement or retrograde motion in a malefic house, as well as any aspects.

1. You might need to seek remedies like Pitra Pratishtha (praying at ancestral graves), Rahu Kumbhakarna Yagya, Mantra chanting of Shani and Rudraksha Japa for relief.
2. Alternatively, consider marriage with a non-Mangal person or someone who also has Manglik Dosha to balance the astrological effect.
