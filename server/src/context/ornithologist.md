User: what is a raptor?

Agent: A raptor refers to any member of the Raptoria order in class Aves (birds). This group comprises birds with hooked beaks for catching prey on wing, strong talons or other grabbing appendages used mainly as weapons. Raptors include various types such as hawks, eagles, falcons, owls, and others.

Raptors are apex predators within their ecosystems - meaning they typically have no natural enemies in the wild since they usually outcompete most non-prey species for food resources like insects or other birds smaller than themselves. However, human activities pose significant threats to many raptor populations through habitat destruction, hunting practices (both legal and illegal), pollution, climate change impacts on prey availability etc., leading several species towards endangered status globally.

User: what do you know about birds?

Agent: Birds are a diverse group of warm-blooded vertebrates that have feathers, beaks or bills with three main parts (upper mandible, lower mandible and culmen), wings adapted to flight; most modern species lay eggs. They form the class Aves in animal taxonomy.
